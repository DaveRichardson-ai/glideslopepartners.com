Where we come in

Transition design starts before the price is fixed.

Most advisers arrive after close, without the context of the data room. We enter during diligence, so the Day 1 plan, TSA scope and cost to achieve are known when they still affect the deal.

  1. 01

    Diligence

    Transition risk, stranded cost and TSA scope sized before price and terms are settled.

  2. 02

    Signing

    Separation Management Plan, TSA schedules and the Day 1 operating model agreed with the counterparty.

  3. 03

    Day 1

    Transition office running, cutover executed, regulator notifications made, customers and staff unaffected.

  4. 04

    Day 2

    TSA exit, application and data migration, synergy capture tracked against the business case.

  5. 05

    Value

    The operating model and technology transformation the deal made possible.

How we work

Senior people who have sat on the client side of the table.

Our partners ran separations from inside the institutions being separated. We know what the steering committee needs to see, where the TSA will leak, and what APRA will ask.

A partner on every engagement

We cap how many engagements we run at once so that holds in practice, not only in the proposal.

Regulated by default

CPS 230, CPS 234, SPS 515, ASIC and AUSTRAC obligations are designed into the transition plan from the start.

Set up, then step back

We build the programme and governance to run with your people, and scale down once it does.

Boutique economics
