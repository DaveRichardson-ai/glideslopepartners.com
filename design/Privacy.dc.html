Glideslope Partners (ABN 19 690 995 585) is an Australian M&A transition and transformation advisory. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through glideslopepartners.com, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. We collect only what we need, and we do not sell personal information.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use the contact form or email us: your name, organisation, email address, the stage of your transaction if you choose to give it, and your message.
  • Site analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across sites. It records aggregate information such as pages viewed, referring sites, country, browser and device type.
  • Security checks. The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated spam. It processes technical signals about your browser for that purpose only.

We do not ask for sensitive information, government identifiers or financial account details through this site.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to reply and, where you ask us to, to discuss an engagement. We use aggregate analytics to understand which pages are useful and to keep the site working well. We do not use your information for any other purpose without your consent, unless the law requires or permits it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that run the site and our email, under confidentiality obligations: Cloudflare (hosting, analytics, spam protection and email routing) and Google (email). Some of these providers may store data outside Australia. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site and the contact form.

Storage and security

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep enquiry emails only as long as we need them to respond and to maintain business records. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a complaint, by emailing hello@glideslopepartners.com. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
