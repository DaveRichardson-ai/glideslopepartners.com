About

Built by practitioners. Delivered by partners.

We ran separations from inside the institutions being separated, and in consulting roles alongside them. Glideslope is the advisory we wanted when we were on the client side of the table.

Why we started

The value lost between the data room and Day 1 is avoidable.

Transition advice tended to be expensive, slow to mobilise, or arrive after close with no context from diligence. By then the price was fixed, the TSAs were signed and the problems were locked in.

Glideslope closes that gap. We enter during diligence, design the transition before close and run Day 1. Where the deal creates a transformation opportunity, we stay to help deliver it.

We also work outside active deals. Private equity firms and corporates engage us on legacy modernisation, platform rationalisation and operating model redesign, with the same people and the same approach.

How we work

We take a small number of engagements at a time, and a founding partner is directly involved in every one. We work alongside your people. We set the programme up, mobilise the governance and step back once it runs, rather than embedding for the duration.
