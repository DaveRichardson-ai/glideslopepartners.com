Putting wealth back into a bank: what the exits teach about re-entry

The bank exits of 2018–2023 cut eight kinds of connection. Any route back in has to decide which to rebuild, rent or leave out.

David Richardson, Managing Partner · [Date] · 8 min read

Wealth is back on the bank agenda

ANZ's FY26 result on 9 November put wealth back on the table for Australian banks. Cash profit rose 31% to A$7.6 billion, and coverage of the result described the next phase of the 2030 strategy as a growth bet, with wealth and the mass-affluent segment at its centre.

That is a reversal. Between 2018 and 2023 every major bank sold or wound down most of its wealth business. The question for any bank now considering re-entry is less whether wealth is attractive and more what it costs to put back together what the exits took apart.

What the exits actually separated

The exits did not remove one business. They cut eight kinds of connection between a bank and its wealth operations.
